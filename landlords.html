Transparent pricing, no upfront fees

For landlords who want a completely hands-off investment.

  • Everything in Tenant-Find, plus:
  • Move-in inventory & check-in
  • Rent collection & monthly statements
  • Dedicated, named property manager
  • 24/7 maintenance line & vetted contractors
  • Routine inspections with reports
  • Full legal & safety compliance handled
  • Deposit dispute & end-of-tenancy management

Enquire about Fully Managed

Why go Fully Managed? Beyond finding your tenant, we handle everything after move-in — rent, maintenance, inspections and compliance — so it more than pays for itself in saved time and fewer voids.

Our homes are let by the room, so fees are charged per room and are subject to VAT. No upfront fees — our fee is only payable on a successful let. Not sure which fits? Talk to us — we'll recommend the right option, no pressure.
