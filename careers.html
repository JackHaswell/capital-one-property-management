The opening

Property Manager

One hands-on Property Manager to take ownership of the day-to-day running of our residential portfolio — keeping properties let, tenants supported, maintenance moving and compliance on track. This is not a narrow admin role.

Location
Darlington, Hartlepool & Teesside · field-based
Hours
24 hrs/week · Mon, Tue & Fri
Pay
£14–£15/hour PAYE + bonuses
Type
Permanent, part-time → full-time

One rule sums up the job: once an issue reaches you, you either resolve it fully or escalate it clearly — you don't forward problems back to the directors.

Why this role is good

Not another cog in a branch

A portfolio you can actually know

If you're running 150 units for a branch, this is the opposite. You take ownership of a manageable portfolio and know every tenant, house and contractor by name.

Real autonomy, direct to the directors

You make the day-to-day decisions inside clear procedures and authority limits. The directors focus on acquisitions and growth — the running of the portfolio is yours.

Built to grow with you

Start part-time while the portfolio's still manageable. As you perform and it grows, so do your hours, earnings and responsibility — with the aim of full-time and helping build the wider team.

What you'll own

The whole cycle, end to end

  • Tenants. Day-to-day tenant and applicant communication — access, rent queries, house rules, first-stage complaints, and move-in and move-out communication.
  • Re-lets. Listings, enquiries, applicant pre-screening, viewings, documentation and move-ins — keeping voids as short as possible.
  • Maintenance. Triage repairs, coordinate approved contractors, arrange access, attend trade meetings, check completed work and keep tenants updated until jobs are closed.
  • Rent & arrears. Monitor missed payments, contact tenants promptly, manage agreed payment plans within policy and escalate serious cases.
  • Compliance. Own the compliance calendar — keep certificates, licences, inspections and scheduled actions up to date.
  • Inspections. Quarterly, with written reports, photographs and follow-up actions.
  • Serviced accommodation. Coordinate changeovers, quality checks and room preparation. Cleaning is not part of the role.
  • Records & systems. Keep COHO and supporting systems accurate, including Inventory Base, SignWell, the property portals and Xero where required.
  • Landlord support. Help with property updates and operational matters as your responsibility grows.
Everything's done in our systems, not on paper — COHO, Inventory Base, SignWell, the property portals and Xero. Experience with property software matters; experience with these exact systems doesn't — we'll train you on ours.
What we need from you

Is this you?

  • Experience in residential lettings or property management, and ready for more ownership
  • A strong practical understanding of tenants, maintenance, re-lets and compliance
  • Excellent organisation and follow-through — nothing needs chasing twice
  • Confidence prioritising urgent and routine work without constant direction
  • Clear written and verbal communication, and calm in difficult conversations
  • Confidence working independently across multiple properties
  • A full UK driving licence, your own vehicle and business-use motor insurance — the patch covers Darlington, Hartlepool and Teesside
HMO management experience is highly desirable but not essential — proven HMO experience qualifies for the upper end of the pay range. Experience with property software is useful, but it doesn't need to be our exact systems.
What you get

The package

£14 to £15 per hour PAYE depending on experience, plus bonuses — on a 24-hour week across Monday, Tuesday and Friday, with the room to grow to full-time.

  • £50 re-let bonus — for each completed re-let.
  • £500 compliance bonus — subject to the defined criteria (pro rata where applicable).
  • £30 per qualifying out-of-hours callout, on a shared rota. Routine messages wait until working hours — you're not on call 24/7.
  • Business mileage at the HMRC approved rate.
  • Company phone and laptop, so you never use your own number or kit.
  • 16.8 days holiday for the three-day pattern, including bank holidays as applicable, plus a workplace pension (subject to eligibility).
  • Structured training and onboarding, with clear operating procedures from day one.
  • Real progression — scope for hours, earnings and responsibility to grow with the portfolio, with the aim of a full-time role and the chance to help build the wider team.
How it works

How the role works

  • Field-based across Darlington, Hartlepool and Teesside, with admin done from home where appropriate.
  • Monday, Tuesday and Friday, 8 hours a day — with reasonable flexibility by agreement around viewings, move-ins and contractor appointments.
  • Working directly with both directors, Joshua Graham & Jack Haswell, with structured onboarding and clear operating procedures.
  • Authority that grows — during probation the directors keep closer oversight of repairs and key decisions; as you complete probation and competence sign-off, more day-to-day authority transfers to you.
  • Permanent PAYE contract with a three-month probation period. Full terms provided before appointment.
