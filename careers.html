Careers · We're hiring

Fill our rooms with the right tenants, fast

Capital One Property Management runs professional house shares and HMOs across Hartlepool, Darlington, Stockton-on-Tees and Middlesbrough. We're building a technology-led lettings operation — a database of pre-vetted tenants, automated room alerts and tenant-to-room matching before anyone views. We're hiring one Lettings Negotiator to own the lettings process end to end: advertise the room, screen the applicants, run the viewings and get the right tenant moved in with the shortest possible void.

The opening

Lettings Negotiator

One Lettings Negotiator to keep our managed rooms let — owning everything from advertisement to move-in, filling vacancies quickly with suitable tenants, and building a pipeline of pre-vetted applicants so every room re-lets with the shortest possible void. It's a sales role at heart: fast response, structured screening and confident closing. Repairs, compliance and rent arrears sit with the directors — your focus is filling rooms.

Location
Hartlepool, Darlington, Stockton & Middlesbrough · home-based + travel
Hours
25 hrs/week · Mon–Fri afternoons
Pay
£15/hour PAYE + letting bonus
Type
Permanent, part-time → full-time

One rule sums up the job: once a matter reaches you, you either resolve it or escalate it clearly with a recommendation — you don't pass problems back to the directors.

Why this role is good

A lettings role built around closing

A tech-led lettings engine

You're not cold-working a list. You draw on a growing database of pre-vetted tenants, automated room alerts and tenant-to-room matching — and you help build these systems as we roll them out.

Earn on every let

A clear letting bonus on top of your hourly pay: £100 for a room let within 7 days, £50 within 14, £25 after. The faster you fill a room, the more you earn.

Built to grow into more

Start part-time while we scale. As the rooms under management grow, so do your hours — with the aim of full-time and a move into business development: winning landlords, meeting clients and building the brand.

What you'll own

The whole lettings cycle, end to end

  • Marketing & listings. Create, publish and maintain room listings on SpareRoom, OpenRent and our own website — to our photography and presentation standard, at rents set by the directors.
  • Enquiries. Respond to every tenant enquiry the same working day, across every channel. Start and end each day with the enquiry inbox cleared.
  • Screening. Run structured telephone screening against our suitability criteria, and progress the right applicants to viewing.
  • Viewings. Schedule and run viewings across the four towns, grouped by location — including after-work slots on set days and Saturday mornings when booked. Confirm, follow up the same day and manage no-shows.
  • Applications & move-ins. Manage referencing through to tenancy agreement, deposit, inventory handover and key release — so every move-in is correct and on the agreed date.
  • Tenant database. Record every enquiry, applicant and former tenant accurately, with the right consent, and re-engage suitable contacts as rooms come up. This database underpins the whole strategy.
  • Pipeline. Keep a pool of pre-vetted applicants ready to move, and build relationships with local employers who need staff accommodation.
  • Reporting. Report weekly on response times, viewings conducted, lets agreed and void days per room.
  • Systems. Keep COHO and our other systems accurate and up to date, and follow our written processes and templates.
Everything runs in our systems, not on paper — COHO, the property portals and our own tenant database. You need to be comfortable with property and messaging software; experience with these exact systems isn't required — we'll train you on ours.
What we need from you

Is this you?

  • Sales experience in a role built on fast response, follow-up and closing — lettings, estate agency, recruitment or telesales
  • Confident and effective on the phone — able to ask direct questions and turn down unsuitable applicants courteously
  • Well organised, with accurate records and the discipline to follow every lead through to completion without being chased
  • Able to work independently across all four towns and manage your own time
  • A full UK driving licence, access to a vehicle for work and business-use motor insurance — or another reliable way to meet the travel the role needs
  • Comfortable with property software and messaging tools — we train you on ours
Residential lettings or HMO experience is desirable, not essential — as is knowledge of the Hartlepool, Darlington, Stockton and Middlesbrough rental markets. If you can sell, respond fast and stay organised, we want to hear from you.
What you get

The package

£15.00 an hour, PAYE, on a 25-hour week across Monday to Friday afternoons — plus a letting bonus on every room you fill, and the room to grow to full-time.

  • Letting bonus on every let — £100 for a room let within 7 days, £50 within 14 days, £25 thereafter (discretionary scheme; full terms provided before you start).
  • Saturday mornings paid as extra — no more than two a month, 9am–1pm, at your normal hourly rate, and only when viewings are booked.

  • 28 days' holiday a year including bank holidays, pro rata to your contracted hours, plus a workplace pension (auto-enrolment).

  • Real progression — scope for hours and earnings to grow with the portfolio, with the aim of a full-time role and a move into business development.
How it works

How the role works

  • Home-based, with travel across Hartlepool, Darlington, Stockton-on-Tees and Middlesbrough for viewings and property visits.
  • 25 hours a week, Monday to Friday — normally 12pm to 5pm, with two days worked 1pm to 6pm for after-work viewings (which two is agreed each week around demand).
  • Occasional Saturday mornings when viewings are booked — no more than two a month, confirmed by the Wednesday before, and paid as extra hours.
  • Working directly with both directors, Joshua Graham & Jack Haswell, with a structured three-week induction and clear operating procedures.
  • Authority that grows — during probation every tenant is approved by a director before an offer is made; more day-to-day authority transfers to you as you settle in.
  • Permanent PAYE contract with a six-month probation and a formal review at three months. Full terms provided before appointment.
  • Start date — as soon as possible, by agreement.
